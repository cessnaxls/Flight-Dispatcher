AeroMission Ops iPad PATCH 5
- Legs/Load has a distinct blue movement header and individual leg tiles.
- Legs/Load format: ICAO -> ICAO (NM), Zulu time, PAX, CARGO.
- Zulu times are calculated server-side from local ISO wall-clock time + IANA timezone using Intl, including DST/non-whole-hour offsets.
- Job and mission prompts require four-letter ICAO identifiers only; invalid airport identifiers are rejected from generated results.
- Job Board difficulty badge no longer appends a percent sign.
- ALL LEVELS explicitly varies difficulty; board generation explicitly varies leg counts from 1-4.
- Each Job Board card includes an operation-category badge.
